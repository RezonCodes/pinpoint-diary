Your privacy

  • Your diary is stored only on this phone and is encrypted with your password.
  • Your location is used only when you save a moment, to look up the place name and weather.
  • No ads, no tracking, no accounts on a server. You can export or delete everything at any time.
  • If you forget your password, your diary cannot be recovered.
